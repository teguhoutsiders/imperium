        Backup

        Save everything in Imperium to one file and keep it somewhere safe, such as Google Drive. You can restore that file here later, for example after a break in your Pro plan, and carry on where you left off.

        Full backup (.zip) holds all your notes, numbers and settings plus every photo and file: payment proofs, cost receipts, dream covers, link logos and everything in Files. Restoring it reconnects each photo to its entry. Data only (.xlsx) is smaller and opens in Excel, but has no photos or files.

        Inside the backup, the Excel sheet named _backup is what Imperium restores from. Don’t edit or delete it.
